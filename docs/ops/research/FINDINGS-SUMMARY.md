## Verified headline statistics (with sources — for the stats hub + citations)

NCMEC CyberTipline:
- 2024: 20.5M reports (vs record 36.2M in 2023; 2023→2024 drop largely a Meta
  report-bundling artifact; ~29.2M distinct incidents in 2024). 62.9M files.
  84% resolved outside the US. Generative-AI reports +1,325% (4,700→67,000).
  Online enticement incl. sextortion +192% (186,000→546,000+; REPORT Act May 2024).
- 2025 "first look": 21.3M reports; online enticement 1.4M (+158%); child sex
  trafficking 113,500+ (+323%); 158,000 files categorized GAI CSAM since 2023.
- ACCURACY NUANCE (Stanford / Riana Pfefferkorn 2025-26): a "Generative AI"-
  flagged CyberTipline report does NOT necessarily mean the file IS AI CSAM —
  the category is ambiguous; avoid conflating flag counts with confirmed AI CSAM.

IWF (2024 Annual Data & Insights, pub Jan 2025):
- 291,270 webpages of confirmed CSAM actioned (record; +5% vs 2023). A report
  assessed every 74 seconds. EU hosts >half of CSAM webpages IWF finds;
  Netherlands most-abused host.
- AI-CSAM: 245 reports actioned (+380% vs 51 in 2023) = 7,644 images; 7,063
  realistic enough to be "real" under UK law; 98% depicted girls; first
  realistic AI CSAM videos confirmed 2024.

Thorn Safer (2024 Impact Report):
- 2024: detected 1,979,406 known + 2,237,225 potential-novel CSAM files
  (4,162,256 total); processed 112.3B files; 60+ platforms. Cumulative since
  2019: 228.8B files processed, 6,475,553 CSAM files detected.
- Framework: "Safety by Design for Generative AI" (Thorn + All Tech Is Human,
  Apr 2024), signed by Amazon, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Microsoft, OpenAI,
  Stability AI, Mistral, Civitai, Metaphysic (Invoke Jul 2024) — the citable
  industry standard for AI labs on child safety.

Tech Coalition Lantern: launched Nov 2023, first cross-platform signal-sharing
