## North star for v3

The v0.5→v2.0 arc made this the **best English-language encyclopedia in the field** — zero P0/P1 factual errors, no orphan pages, every crisis link live. v3's north star is the harder half of "best on the planet": be the resource a distressed person **in any country reaches first**, and the source that **journalists, clinicians, educators, and courts cite by default** — found by the real words people type, believed because every claim is named-reviewed and dated, usable in seconds on any device and in more than one language, and reusable by allied institutions — all without ever tracking or endangering a visitor. The single unifying theme: **from "the best encyclopedia" to "the fastest, most trusted, most reachable, most reusable help on the planet" — legible to humans, machines, and allies alike.**

## Top 5 bets for v3

1. **Internationalize the crisis path with correct local hotlines (v2.5 groundwork → v3.0).** This is the single biggest multiplier on "best on the planet." The content is excellent but English/US-only, while the harm is global; a panicking sextortion victim in Madrid or a parent in Taipei cannot parse a second language *or* dial a US 800-number. The i18n PM's sharpest insight is decisive: **translated words around a dead US number still fail the person in crisis** — so the hotline registry (decoupling numbers from US hardcoding, expert-verified, no geolocation) is as important as the translation itself, and both must ship together. Largest reach, and the work that genuinely converts a US resource into a world resource.

2. **The typed data spine: JSON-LD schema + citation store + reviewer registry + honest dates (v2.1 → v2.5).** Treated separately by the PMs but it is *one* compounding asset. Schema makes the site legible to search engines, citation managers, and AI answer engines (so they cite us and preserve the ICD-11 framing instead of stripping it); the citation store makes "every claim is sourced" a build-enforced invariant a court or journalist can click through; named reviewer bylines convert trust from an unverifiable claim into an accountable fact. Each is the structured projection of the same data, so building the spine once pays off across Reach *and* Trust for the life of the site.

3. **`/glossary` + FAQ + breadcrumbs — the discoverability wedge (v2.2).** Definitional and "how I feel" queries ("what is grooming", "is my kid addicted to porn", "someone is blackmailing me") are exactly what journalists, educators, and distressed users type, and definition/FAQ pages are the web's most-linked, most-featured-snippet content type. The glossary is also a durable backlink asset (courts and educators cite glossaries) and the terminology backbone that locks survivor-centered language site-wide. Modest effort, retires two audit items, and compounds into both citations and crisis-query reach.

4. **Crisis-grade UX foundation: Quick Exit + a11y sweep + accessible mobile nav + crisis-red (v2.1).** Cheap, fast, and they fix the exact failure modes that hurt the people the site exists for. Quick Exit closes a real **physical-safety** gap (today there is zero escape affordance for a monitored sextortion victim or abuse survivor); the a11y sweep unblocks screen-reader and keyboard users on a site that currently exposes 71 decorative arrows and has only 3 aria-labels; the mobile-nav fix matters because the majority of crisis traffic is mobile. These build trust, unblock i18n (the `ExternalLink` component is a prerequisite), and should ship first.

5. **Stabilize delivery + automated quality gate (v2.5).** For a resource people rely on in emergencies, "it looked fine when I shipped it" is not good enough, and shipping prod from one laptop with no pre-deploy gate is a standing liability. Reconnecting/hardening deploy plus a thin Playwright+axe+content-invariant harness and perf budgets means a PR that breaks the triage flow, drops a hotline number, regresses contrast, or tanks load time on a slow phone is caught **before** it reaches a person in crisis. It is the foundation that makes every other bet safe to ship at pace.

## Deferred / won't-do

- **Naming the porn-science skeptics — REJECTED (permanent owner policy).** v3 states the ICD-11 official position (CSBD = impulse-control disorder), excludes the skeptics framing, and never underplays the imperative to help people struggling with porn use *or* CSAM. The V2 audit's P1 #7 ("name the skeptic studies") predates and is overridden by this decision; the `/methodology` page (v2.5) documents the exclusion neutrally as an editorial *scope* choice, never as "naming skeptics." **Do not reintroduce.**
- **Survivor / lived-experience "Voices" content layer (content I7) — deferred; only with extreme care, not a v3 sprint.** High human value but the highest ethical risk in the whole council (re-traumatization, consent, sensationalism). If ever pursued: public-only/consented sourcing, mandatory content warnings, persistent crisis affordances, **no solicitation of new disclosures**, owner sign-off per item — and if consent/sourcing can't be cleared cleanly, ship clinician/advocate framing only. Not scheduled in v3.
- **Full headless-CMS authoring migration (platform I6, full scope) — deferred; pilot only.** XL effort and the highest regret risk for a small maintainer; a CMS can introduce its own breakage. **Do** the thin pilot inside the data spine — move volatile bits (hotline numbers, statistics, org list) to schema-validated typed data files with the CI gates (already implicit in the citation store + hotline registry). Defer the actual CMS decision until that loop is proven.
- **Individual-level tracking / ad-tech analytics — won't-do.** Any measurement must be privacy-preserving, cookieless, crisis-route-excluded, and is an explicit owner decision (Open Questions), never an assumed default. "No analytics at all" is an acceptable outcome.
- **User accounts / login / saved profiles — out of scope.** Storing personal data about visitors to this subject matter is a liability and a safety risk. The triage "save a plan" feature must be **local/offline-only**, never server-stored.
- **Community forum / user-generated content / comments — out of scope.** Moderation burden and abuse risk are disqualifying for a small-maintainer project in this domain. (The "Voices" layer above is explicitly curated, not UGC.)
- **PNG rasterization for fact cards / native-binary image deps — deferred.** Ship SVG-first; add satori+resvg only on clear partner demand, since native binaries complicate the build.
- **Email newsletter — deferred behind RSS.** RSS is the privacy-cleanest channel and ships in v2.5. Email only if real demand, double-opt-in, a provider that clears the no-tracking bar, low-frequency; if none clears it, RSS only.

## Execution plan (build phase)

This roadmap was produced by a **read-only Product Council workflow** — proposals + synthesis only, zero write-race risk. The *build* runs only after the owner approves and answers the Open Questions. The hard lesson from v0.5→v2.0 is baked in: **the two-writer race corrupts `main`** (see `docs/ops/ENVIRONMENT-NOTES-2026-05-31.md` — edits that reported success but didn't persist, a guard that broke every build). Therefore:

**Worktree-per-release (or per large initiative), single-writer rule.**
- One git worktree per sub-release: `git worktree add ../addiction-v2.1 -b v2.1`, etc. Each worktree has **exactly one writing agent**. Never two agents editing files in the same checkout. For large independent initiatives within a release (e.g. the citation-store backfill vs the fuzzy-search rebuild), give each its own worktree.
- Read-only fan-out (research, audits, link-checks, this council) is safe to parallelize freely.

**Per-initiative loop (every writer follows this):**
1. `main == origin/main` confirmed (compare SHAs via `node -e` + `execSync`, not bash display) before branching.
2. Make the change. **Verify with node, not bash text** — the environment intermittently garbles grep/cat/Read display (a 391-line file once reported as 32,721 lines). Use `node -e "fs.readFileSync(...)"` for structure.
3. `npm run build` green — the `prebuild` guards must pass. **Every new route registered in `lib/search.ts` PAGE_REGISTRY** (the search-registry guard fails otherwise), and any new prebuild guard (`check-jsonld`, `check-review-coverage`, `check-citation-integrity`, `check-embed-isolation`, plus wiring `lint:perishable`) added to the chain.
4. Commit; **confirm the edit actually landed** via `git show HEAD:<file>` piped to `grep -c` (the reliable check), not a bare working-file grep.
5. Merge to main single-writer; **manual deploy:** `vercel deploy --prod --yes --scope team_65sJgKaWhC9KAkvcEl9afm3x` (git auto-deploy is broken until the v2.5 deploy-stabilization initiative fixes it).
6. Verify live with a **browser User-Agent** (prod is behind a Vercel bot challenge — bare curl gets a 403) and assert the page **title/content** appears in the response. Remember `&` renders as `&amp;` (search for "Laws" not "Laws & Policy").

**Parallelizable vs serial.**
- *Parallel-safe (different files, independent):* every v2.1 quick win is its own worktree (Quick Exit, a11y sweep, mobile nav, crisis-color, related-help block, accuracy fixes); in v2.2 glossary, `/platform-safety`, youth-exposure stats, therapy guide, and OG cards are mutually independent.
- *Serial / dependency-ordered:* the **JSON-LD schema helper (v2.1) must precede** FAQ, breadcrumbs, glossary schema, and citation export. **Honest dates (v2.1) precede** changelog + cite-kit + chapter schema. The **citation store precedes** the currency engine's richest form and the dataset/API. **`[lang]` routing precedes** all translation; the **hotline registry precedes** Triage v2's region hook and the embed widget; **Triage v2 precedes** nothing but reuses print/offline. **Fix nav label drift before breadcrumbs** (or breadcrumbs inherit the drift). The **deploy/CI harness (v2.5)** should land before the v3.0 flagship so the largest changes ship behind a green gate.

**Suggested cadence:** spin a worktree + writer per v2.1 quick win, barrier-merge them behind one build-green check, then proceed release by release. Run a read-only verification agent (browser-UA fetch asserting titles) after each merge to confirm live state. Update `docs/ops/v3-roadmap.md` and `PLAN.md` handoff logs as each initiative lands so the next agent can resume cold.
